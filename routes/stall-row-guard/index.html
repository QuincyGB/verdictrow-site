Guard

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/guard

$0.01 USDC per call · 10000 atomic units (Base accept)

Guard — one pay / caution / avoid verdict for one x402 endpoint, composed from the row's inspector tools: the live 402 sign read and SDK-checked, the payTo screened against the bad-payTo registry (OFAC plus rotation/vanishing flags, evidence attached), and the resource's recorded history — unknown history caps the verdict at CAUTION. Every verdict carries its reasons; the one probe inside is unpaid. Use this when your agent is about to pay an endpoint it does not know.
