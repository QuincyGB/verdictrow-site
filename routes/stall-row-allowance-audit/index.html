Allowance Audit

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/allowance-audit

$0.005 USDC per call · 5000 atomic units (Base accept)

Audit one Base wallet's ERC-20 approvals against a curated list of major spenders (Permit2, Uniswap, Aerodrome, aggregators): active approvals are listed and unlimited ones flagged high risk. Use this when an agent or its owner wants to know what a wallet has already authorized before it holds or moves funds. When the explorer cannot supply the held-token list, the token-dependent fields come back marked unknown rather than the call failing.
