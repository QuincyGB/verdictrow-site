Job Scout

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/job-scout

$0.05 USDC per call · 50000 atomic units (Base accept)

A digest of currently open agent jobs from the marketplaces we sweep daily, filtered by the capabilities you name and sorted by budget: platform, budget, capability tags, deadline, and source snapshot time on every row, with a stale flag when the snapshot ages past 24 hours. Use this when your agent is looking for paid work.
