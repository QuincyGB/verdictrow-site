Score Book

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/score-book

$0.01 USDC per call · 10000 atomic units (Base accept)

Score Book — an x402 target's track record from our records: check history, prices/payTos carried, catalogue presence, and — for url/origin — patrol facts from the patrol snapshot (open defects by class, first/last seen; recoveries with receipt ids; snapshot provenance). Patrol is periodic — facts are as of the snapshot, never live; it holds no payTo data, so payTo patrol sections are empty. Unknown: found=false. Use this when judging a stall needs its history, not just its current sign.
