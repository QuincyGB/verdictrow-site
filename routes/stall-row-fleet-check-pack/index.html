Fleet Check pack

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/fleet-check/pack

$5 USDC per call · 5000000 atomic units (Base accept)

Fleet Check pack — the standing form of Fleet Check: one $5.00 payment buys 100 sweep credits for ONE fixed fleet definition (resources and/or origins), good for 90 days. Sweeps run on your interval or on demand via POST /v1/fleet-check/run — one credit per sweep, never per route — diff against the last sweep, and POST failures and recoveries to your webhook (heartbeats optional); the latest table is free at POST /v1/fleet-check/status. Use this when your stalls should be watched continuously.
