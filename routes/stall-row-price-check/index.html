Price Check

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/price-check

$0.05 USDC per call · 50000 atomic units (Base accept)

Pricing comps for an x402 listing, read live from the CDP Bazaar public catalog: how many resources match your keywords, their price distribution, the closest comparables, and a recommended listing price with its rationale. Use this when you operate an x402 route and are setting or changing its price.
