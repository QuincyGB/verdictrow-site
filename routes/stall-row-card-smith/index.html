Card Smith

StallRow · POST https://stall-row-production.up.railway.app/v1/card-smith

$0.1 USDC per call · 100000 atomic units (Base accept)

Generate or grade an agent card (the /.well-known/agent-card.json an agent publishes for A2A and AgentWorld-style registries). Generate builds a structurally valid card from your fields; grade scores a supplied card and itemizes its defects. Use this when you are registering an agent and want the card right the first time.
