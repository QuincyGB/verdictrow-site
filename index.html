How the patrol works

STEP 1

Read the signs

Discovery documents, schemas and payment rails are probed as a buyer meets them — a live 402 at the listed price, decodable and payable, or the defect goes in the book.

STEP 2

Keep the books

Scores, defects and recoveries are kept in the open: the Score Book, the patrol ledger (887 routes watched) and a signed, hash-chained change feed anyone can audit.

STEP 3

Issue the receipt

Verdicts ship with their evidence — deduction lists, probe results, settlement records — so a buyer never has to take the patrol's word for anything.

Building an inspector? The fleet has a client SDK — npm install @quincygb/verdict-row-inspector · npm · GitHub. Free sample responses for every route are linked from the tools index.
